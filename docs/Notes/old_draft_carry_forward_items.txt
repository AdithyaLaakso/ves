Possible Carry-Forward Items from Older Draft

Source compared:
- current main draft: `docs/ves_proposal.odt`
- older draft: `docs/Drafts/vesuvius_uncial_proposal.odt`

Conclusion:

Nothing crucial appears to have been lost wholesale, but several specific arguments from the older draft may still be worth reconsidering during redrafting.

1. Thucydides as a poor default textual prior

The older draft argued that Thucydides was not an ideal default textual prior for likely Herculaneum content, since the villa corpus is more plausibly Epicurean or philosophical in character.

Why it may still matter:
- It sharpens the domain-validity argument.
- It shows that textual assumptions, not only image assumptions, may be historically misaligned.
- It may be useful if the paper still discusses likely target content or linguistic priors.

2. Stronger preprocessing sentence

The older draft stated clearly that no single global preprocessing recipe works reliably across all samples, and that hard thresholding or aggressive cleaning can destroy useful grayscale structure.

Why it may still matter:
- This is a concise and strong formulation.
- It may be worth restoring if the current wording on preprocessing feels too softened or diffuse.

3. Explicit link to Niccolardi on variable readability across image versions

The older draft connected the preprocessing point to the observation that different versions of the same image can provide different readability.

Why it may still matter:
- The current Niccolardi notes support this strongly.
- It could strengthen the argument against overcommitting to a single image representation or single preprocessing path.

4. Modern punctuation / monotonic versus polytonic detail

The older draft was more explicit about the mismatch between modern Greek written conventions and ancient materials, including monotonic accents, punctuation conventions, and related inherited but altered forms.

Why it may still matter:
- This can sharpen the argument that modern Greek handwriting or text conventions are not straightforward proxies for ancient Greek materials.
- It may be useful if the current treatment of domain mismatch feels too compressed.

What does not need to be carried forward:

- internal team history
- Discord or communication history
- project leadership assumptions
- comments about who lost interest or remained engaged

Those were appropriate to an earlier internal draft but are not needed in the current proposal.
