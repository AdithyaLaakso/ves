Side-by-Side Rationale Table

Issue | Synthetic-Only Pipeline | Revised Manifest-Based Pipeline
----- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------
Training source | Fully synthetic or heavily synthetic pairs | Real ALPUB_v2 uncial images
Main strength | High control over noise and corruption | Better domain alignment to actual target forms
Main risk | Model may learn simulator assumptions/artifacts | Real data are messier and more imbalanced
Morphology | Depends on what the generator approximates | Comes from authentic uncial exemplars
Noise model | Explicitly controlled | Controlled degradation still available, but applied to real images
Class balance | Can be made artificially uniform | Real class distribution preserved
Imbalance handling | Often solved by synthetic equalization | Mild sampler weighting without changing corpus contents
Validation leakage risk | Depends on how generated data are split | Reduced via document-level splitting
Historical defensibility | Weaker once real uncial data exist | Stronger because supervision is closer to target domain
Best use | Bootstrap when real domain data are unavailable | Primary training path once relevant corpus is available

Summary:

The revised pipeline keeps the controllability of synthetic corruption but relocates supervision onto a more historically relevant real-image corpus.
